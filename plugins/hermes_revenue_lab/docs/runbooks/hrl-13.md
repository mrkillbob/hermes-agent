# HRL-13 Human Approval Boundary Runbook

## Current classification

HRL-13 is **acceptance-valid infrastructure**. No approval signing key and no approval grants are
installed by this patch, so consequential actions remain unavailable.

`config/approval_policy.json` is the machine-readable boundary. It requires human approval for:

- creating marketplace accounts;
- accepting new terms;
- spending money;
- publishing the first product in a category;
- starting paid advertising;
- sending a novel outbound campaign;
- materially changing prices;
- subscribing to paid APIs;
- issuing refunds;
- entering contracts;
- handling sensitive customer data.

Unknown mutations also require approval. Only deterministic metrics, public-policy reading, private
draft generation, and dry runs are initially classified as local-safe.

## Receipt contract

An approval is valid only when all of the following are true:

- its SHA-256 request binding exactly matches the action, target, and canonical parameters;
- the approver is listed in the policy;
- the receipt is active and unexpired;
- its HMAC validates under an operator-held key of at least 128 bits.

Changing a target, spend ceiling, currency, campaign, category, or any other bound parameter creates
a different request hash and requires a different receipt. A record merely claiming
`approver: owner`, including one generated by a model or agent, is not permission.

## Current operating posture

There is intentionally no approval-creation CLI in this patch. Approval signing must eventually be
owned by an authenticated operator surface and persisted in an append-only, consumable ledger.
Until that exists, the gate can generate stable request hashes and report approval requirements but
cannot autonomously satisfy them.

The approval boundary does not replace HRL-12 compliance. An action must pass compliance first and
then pass this approval gate; human approval cannot legalize or permit an otherwise blocked action.

## Verification

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src:. PYTEST_ADDOPTS='-p no:cacheprovider' \
  python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_approval_boundary.py
```

The tests mutate scope, expiry, approver identity, signature presence, and policy completeness. No
account action, terms acceptance, publication, payment, outreach, refund, contract, customer-data
handling, or model call occurs.
